# Geometry and Measurement

Reason about congruence, similarity, transformations, circles, right-triangle
trigonometry, coordinate geometry, area, volume, units, and short deductive
geometric arguments.

## Scope

- Subject: `mathematics`
- Learner level: `foundational`; designed for a motivated adult self-learner.
- Confirmed deck prerequisite: `mathematics/elementary-algebra-and-functions`.
- Confirmed inbound capabilities from the resolved closure: whole-number and
  signed arithmetic; fractions and decimals; ratios, rates, proportions, and
  percents; measurement, unit conversion, precision, and reasonableness;
  variables, expressions, equations as equality statements, substitution, and
  expression evaluation.
- Assumed tools: none. Straightedge, compass, ruler, protractor, graph paper,
  and tracing paper are representations introduced when used, not inbound
  software requirements.
- What mastery enables: interpret and construct standard geometric diagrams;
  reason from definitions and transformations; establish and apply congruence
  and similarity; calculate and check plane and solid measures with units;
  solve right triangles; use coordinates for distance, midpoint, slope, and
  short geometric arguments; and build bounded geometric models.
- Deliberate exclusions: formal logic and general proof methods belong to
  `mathematical-reasoning-and-proof`; circular functions, identities, conics,
  polar/parametric descriptions, vectors, and laws of sines and cosines belong
  to `precalculus-and-trigonometry`; equation-heavy analytic geometry waits for
  the unfinished algebra prerequisite; non-Euclidean, projective, and advanced
  synthetic geometry remain deferred by the subject roadmap. Long proofs,
  multi-step design projects, and physical fabrication remain outside SRS.

The current prerequisite snapshot contains only the first scheduled algebra
chapter. This curriculum therefore does not assume general equation-solving,
function graphing, radicals, or line/conic equations. Geometry-specific square
roots are established with the Pythagorean theorem, and coordinate work is
bounded to the available arithmetic, ratio, and substitution capabilities.

## Chapter map

| File | Topic | Durable learner capability |
|---|---|---|
| `01_geometric_language_and_measurement.md` | Geometric language and measurement | Read and produce point, line, ray, segment, angle, distance, and construction notation while distinguishing a diagram from the mathematical claim it represents. |
| `02_angle_relationships_and_parallel_lines.md` | Angle relationships and parallel lines | Use angle pairs, perpendicularity, parallel lines, and transversals to determine measures and give short reasoned justifications. |
| `03_transformations_and_symmetry.md` | Transformations and symmetry | Perform and compose translations, reflections, and rotations; identify invariants and symmetries on paper and on a coordinate grid. |
| `04_triangle_congruence_and_reasoning.md` | Triangle congruence and reasoning | Classify triangles, decide congruence from rigid motion and valid criteria, reject insufficient data, and derive elementary triangle facts. |
| `05_similarity_dilations_and_scale.md` | Similarity, dilations, and scale | Recognize and construct dilations, justify similarity, use corresponding proportions, and interpret scale drawings and scale factors. |
| `06_plane_measurement_and_area.md` | Plane measurement and area | Derive, choose, and apply perimeter and area relationships for polygons and composite regions while tracking linear versus square units and scale. |
| `07_circles_arcs_and_sectors.md` | Circles, arcs, and sectors | Relate radius, diameter, circumference, area, central/inscribed angles, arcs, sectors, tangents, and proportional circle measures. |
| `08_right_triangles_and_trigonometry.md` | Right triangles and trigonometry | Use the Pythagorean theorem, square roots as lengths, and sine/cosine/tangent ratios from similarity to solve and check right triangles. |
| `09_coordinate_geometry_and_geometric_proof.md` | Coordinate geometry and geometric proof | Compute distance and midpoint, transform coordinates, use slope criteria, and justify elementary geometric properties algebraically. |
| `10_surface_area_volume_and_modeling.md` | Surface area, volume, and modeling | Interpret nets and cross-sections, derive and apply surface-area and volume formulas, track cubic scale, and criticize simplified geometric models. |
